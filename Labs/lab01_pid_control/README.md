# Lab 01 — PID Control

> **ROB-UY 2004: Robotic Manipulation & Locomotion** | NYU Tandon School of Engineering

Implementation of a PID (Proportional-Integral-Derivative) controller applied to the Stanford Pupper v3 quadruped robot in simulation.

---

## 🎯 Objective

Design and tune a PID controller to regulate joint positions and achieve stable, precise motor control on a legged robot — the foundation for all higher-level locomotion tasks.

---

## 🧠 Concepts Covered

- **Proportional (P):** Corrects error proportionally to its magnitude
- **Integral (I):** Eliminates steady-state error by accumulating past errors
- **Derivative (D):** Dampens oscillations by reacting to the rate of change of error
- **Tuning:** Adjusting Kp, Ki, Kd gains to balance speed, stability, and overshoot
